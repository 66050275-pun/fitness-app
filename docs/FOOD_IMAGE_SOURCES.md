# Food images

- `thai-dishes.png` and `protein-foods.png` are AI-generated illustrative contact sheets created for this app. They are examples of a dish or ingredient, not photographs of the user's meal or a branded product.
- The individual `.webp` illustrations in `src/assets/food` come from [Culinary Assets v0.5.0](https://github.com/cosmintrica/culinary-assets/tree/v0.5.0), which dedicates its artwork to [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The source's license notice is included in the same directory. These images are AI-generated illustrations.
- Open Food Facts product thumbnails are provided by its contributors under [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/); the product link gives access to the source and credits. The food database attribution and ODbL notice are separate. Only allowlisted HTTPS product-thumbnail URLs are shown, and only in the current online search results.
